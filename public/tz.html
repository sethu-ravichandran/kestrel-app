How routing works now

Old round robin sent every booking to “whoever is next”. A Seattle prospect could land with a New York rep at 6am, and an existing customer would meet a stranger. Halden's ops team was fixing this by hand.

Routing rules · Halden Group

Rules run top to bottom. The first rule that finds an available rep wins, and round robin only breaks ties within that group. Reorder or switch rules off, then check the simulator.

Clock-change guardian

The US, Europe and Australia change their clocks on different dates. For a few weeks each year the gap between two offices shifts by an hour, and that's when "meeting moved on its own" tickets spike. The guardian scans upcoming bookings for that window and fixes them before anyone shows up at the wrong time.
